How people move

TOFUSigned up or installed, no ad made yet. Up to 4 emails, each with one follow-up, then stop.
MOFU 2.1 / 2.32.1: made 1 or 2 ads. 2.3: imported products but no ad yet. 2 emails each, with follow-ups.
BOFU 3.1Used all 3 free generations, not paid. Video clone or out-of-free, then call, then plan pick.
PaidUpgraded. Leaves this funnel the next sync.
A · runs Meta adsEmails built on their own live ads; MOFU and BOFU get generated visuals and the free video clone.
B · store, no Meta adsTemplate picks for their products, Ad Radar winners, a video script. No generated visuals.
U · no store on fileSame as B, without anything store-specific; winners come from across Ad Radar.

The stage is re-read from Ad Studio usage every day, so someone who makes an ad moves from TOFU to MOFU and starts that sequence at the next slot. The furthest stage wins. 10% of every stage is held out and never emailed, to measure the real lift.

The rules

Mon + ThuLocal morning: Europe 08:00 UTC, Americas 14:00 UTC, Asia-Pacific 22:00 UTC the day before.
Max 2 a weekAt least 3 days apart, counting the other programs in the same Brevo account.
One follow-up3 to 10 days after each high-value email, same thread, only if they did not click or reply.
TOFU stops at 44 main emails (plus their follow-ups) with no click, reply or ad made, then nothing more.
A reply pauses them7 days for the reply agent to talk; the call email is dropped since the agent offers the call.
ExitsUpgrade, unsubscribe, "stop" in a reply, a billing or complaint reply (goes to Farooq), bounce or blocklist.
Checks before sendCopy lint and a fact-check on every draft; image QA on generated visuals; anything failing is skipped, the next email goes instead.
SafetyBrevo blocklist check per send, one-click unsubscribe, auto-pause at 2% bounces or 0.1% complaints, daily cap.
PriorityFollow-ups first, then BOFU, MOFU, TOFU; ad runners first; most recent activity first.

What changes when they act

TOFU · A: clicks the first email, then makes an ad

  1. Mon, 23 Nov sends Ad read (TOFU)
  2. Thu, 26 Nov clicks a link in email 1
  3. Thu, 26 Nov sends Winners to clone (TOFU)
  4. Mon, 30 Nov makes their first ad in Ad Studio (moves to MOFU 2.1)
  5. Mon, 30 Nov sends 2 new versions of your ad (MOFU 2.1)
  6. Thu, 03 Dec sends ↩ follow-up to 2 new versions of your ad (MOFU 2.1)
  7. Mon, 07 Dec sends Next templates (MOFU 2.1)
  8. Thu, 10 Dec sends ↩ follow-up to Next templates (MOFU 2.1)
  9. Sequence done: nothing more until their stage changes.

MOFU 2.1 · A: keeps generating, uses all 3 free, then upgrades

  1. Mon, 23 Nov sends 2 new versions of your ad (MOFU 2.1)
  2. Thu, 26 Nov sends ↩ follow-up to 2 new versions of your ad (MOFU 2.1)
  3. Mon, 30 Nov sends Next templates (MOFU 2.1)
  4. Thu, 03 Dec uses all 3 free generations (moves to BOFU)
  5. Thu, 03 Dec sends Free video clone (BOFU 3.1)
  6. Mon, 07 Dec sends ↩ follow-up to Free video clone (BOFU 3.1)
  7. Thu, 10 Dec sends 30 minute call (BOFU 3.1)
  8. Mon, 14 Dec upgrades to a paid plan
  9. Upgraded: leaves the funnel.

BOFU 3.1 · A: replies to the video email, books a call

  1. Mon, 23 Nov sends Free video clone (BOFU 3.1)
  2. Thu, 26 Nov replies with a question (the reply agent answers)
  3. Thu, 03 Dec sends Plan pick (BOFU 3.1)
  4. Sequence done: nothing more until their stage changes.

TOFU · B: unsubscribes after email 2

  1. Mon, 23 Nov sends Templates for you (TOFU)
  2. Thu, 26 Nov sends ↩ follow-up to Templates for you (TOFU)
  3. Mon, 30 Nov unsubscribes
  4. Unsubscribed: never emailed again.

MOFU 2.3 · B: replies "not interested"

  1. Mon, 23 Nov sends Templates for your products (MOFU 2.3)
  2. Thu, 26 Nov replies "not interested" (agent stops them)
  3. Asked to stop: unsubscribed by the reply agent.

Video clones

Made fully in the cloud for a boot shop: their black suede slouch boots, recreated in the style of a 12th Tribe video that has run 499 days. 15 seconds, vertical, with sound. Took 9 minutes.

Main path: cloud

  1. The Worker finds the store's product page (their ad links, a collection they promote, or their catalog) and the product photos.
  2. It picks the best-fitting video winner from a pool of long-running Ad Radar videos (12 categories).
  3. Gemini looks at the product and the winner and writes a 15 second, 2 to 3 shot plan: same look, pacing and mood, their product in every shot, no on-screen text.
  4. Seedance 2.0 renders it through the Ad Studio API on the Brevo account, with the winner as the reference video and the product photos as reference images.
  5. Every 10 minutes the Worker collects finished videos into our storage, writes the store's video email and swaps it in for the call email in any queued slot.

Fallback: Farooq's Chrome

If a cloud render fails or times out (90 minutes), the job moves to the Chrome loop, which runs Ad Studio's own clone agent in the signed-in PushOwl account (it adds text and an end card). If that also fails twice, the store gets the call email first. Out of credits on the API: cloud starts pause, jobs stay queued.

Who gets one: BOFU stores that run Meta ads (255, minus 49 with no usable product page). One per store, about 50 to 60 Ad Studio credits each.
