1TL;DR

  1. Goal: turn Ad Studio sign-ups into paying users.Every email is a short personal note from Farooq that comes with something made for that store. The strongest: their closest competitor's live ad, rebuilt with their own product in Ad Studio.
  2. 120,247 people today, each from the brand they know.Brevo sender (campaigns@m.brevo.com): 39,072 Ad Studio sign-ups and Brevo ecom stores. PushOwl sender (sheik.farooq@pushowl.com): 81,175 PushOwl installs and Ad Studio accounts that came through PushOwl. New sign-ups join every day.
  3. Smart timing, never spammy.Every weekday at 8am local, but each person gets at most 2 a week, 3 days apart, and 1 follow-up only if they did not click. New sign-ups go first, then whoever is closest to buying. It stops the moment they upgrade, unsubscribe or ask.
  4. Replies handled for you.Every reply lands in Farooq's PushOwl inbox; an AI agent answers it in the same thread within about 5 minutes, in their language. Nothing is forwarded to you.
  5. 100% cloud, measured, self-protecting.Runs on Cloudflare and Google Apps Script, nothing on a laptop. A 10% holdout measures the real lift, sending slows to small batches above 3% bounces, and a dashboard with a live reply inbox tracks it all.

4End-to-end flow

CollectAd Studio sign-ups and PushOwl installs (Brevo account), Brevo ecom stores (Omni). Daily.

PlanSun and Wed: who gets which email on Mon or Thu, by priority, caps and their last actions.
MakeClaude writes the note from real facts; Ad Studio makes images and video clones.
CheckCopy lint and a fact-check against the store data. A failing email is never sent; the next one goes.
Send8am local, from the matching sender, with one-click unsubscribe and a branded footer.

AdaptClick or reply: no follow-up. New stage: new sequence. Upgrade or stop: out.

9Video clones

Made fully in the cloud: a boot shop's product in the style of a winning video that ran 499 days. 15 seconds, vertical, with sound.

  • For BOFU ad runners: a finished video they can run today, as a thank you.
  • The Worker finds their product page and photos, and picks the closest long-running winner from Ad Radar.
  • Gemini plans a 15 second shot list; Seedance 2.0 renders it through the Ad Studio API.
  • Every 10 minutes finished videos are stored and the video email replaces the call email in the queue.
  • Fallback only: a job the cloud cannot finish in 90 minutes goes to the Chrome loop on Farooq's Mac.
  • Today: 140 done (118 in the cloud), 56 queued.

10Replies: answered, never forwarded

  • Replies go to sheik.farooq+ads@pushowl.com. Every 5 minutes a Google Apps Script in that mailbox sends new replies to the Worker.
  • The agent classifies, answers from the Ad Studio knowledge base in their language, and replies in the same thread from sheik.farooq@pushowl.com. Up to 8 replies per thread.
  • "Stop", complaints and privacy requests: unsubscribed at once. Billing and refunds: pointed to the in-app chat that can see payments. Partnerships: the call link.

How an email is written

  • Context per person: store facts, their live ads and stats, Ad Radar winners in their category, templates tagged for their category, what they made in Ad Studio, earlier emails, language.
  • Claude Haiku 5.5 writes subject, preview, 5 short paragraphs and one closing line in the voice rules (no hype words, no reply asks, one emoji in the subject).
  • Copy lint (length, links, banned words, repeated lines) and a fact-check against the context. A failing email is retried once, then skipped for the next kind.
  • Links go through the router, which logs the click and opens Ad Studio with the template, ad or video loaded.

Senders and deliverability

  • PushOwl account sender 5: "Farooq from Ad Studio" <sheik.farooq@pushowl.com>. Brevo CRM account: "Farooq from Ad Studio" <campaigns@m.brevo.com>. Both authenticated; seeds pass DKIM, SPF and DMARC and land in the inbox.
  • Reply-To always sheik.farooq+ads@pushowl.com. List-Unsubscribe with one-click POST. Blocklists of both accounts and the transactional blocklist checked before every send.
  • Template: table-based, 600px, Gmail and Outlook safe, logos hosted on our router, plain-text part with the same footer.

Safety rails

  • Nothing sends while send_mode is draft; every send re-checks unsubscribes, stage, blocklists, weekly cap and the follow-up rule right before it goes.
  • Idempotency keys per slot, person and kind: no double sends on retries.
  • Above 3% bounces or 0.1% complaints, sending drops to small hourly batches (no pause); internal and staff domains excluded; 10% holdout never emailed.
  • Visual spend capped at $1,000 a month; video clone credits watched, renders pause when credits run out.
