The short version

124,355sendable contacts in the PushOwl Brevo account
100k+Brevo ecom store connections (Omni), ~100 new a day
~2%sign-up to paid today (PostHog, 60 days). The number this funnel moves.
  • Who: Brevo ecom sign-ups and PushOwl installs, split into stores that run Meta ads (A), stores that do not (B), and people with no store on file (U).
  • What they get: a short, warm note from Farooq that hands them something already made or picked for their store: a template, an Ad Radar winner to clone, a video script, a remade ad. Every link opens Ad Studio with it loaded.
  • From: "Farooq from Ad Studio" <sheik.farooq@pushowl.com>, one to one through the PushOwl Brevo account. Replies are answered in Farooq's Gmail under a hidden label.
  • When: at most 2 emails a week (Mon, Thu), one follow-up after each value email, stop on upgrade or unsubscribe.
  • Spend: image and video generation capped at $1,000 a month and only for ad runners in the middle or bottom of the funnel. Text AI is uncapped.

Order we start in

  1. Active Ad Studio users first (about 2.5k in MOFU/BOFU who used Ad Studio in the last 90 days). Closest to paying.
  2. Daily new sign-ups and installs from day one of sending.
  3. Dormant Ad Studio users (about 19k), in daily batches.
  4. TOFU backlog: only people active in the last 90 days (Ad Studio activity, PushOwl last seen, or email engagement), in daily batches over about 2 weeks. Never the whole list at once.
  5. Brevo ecom stores from Omni, newest connections first.
Batches start small and grow only while bounces stay under 2% and complaints under 0.1%. Either limit crossed pauses sending automatically.

Daily (new sign-ups and installs)

At 03:00 UTC the Worker pulls contacts changed since the last run from the PushOwl Brevo account and yesterday's new store connections from Omni. It finds the store domain, runs the Meta ads check within the hour, assigns stage and cohort, and the person enters the funnel at the next Mon/Thu slot.

Meta ads qualification

How it works

  • Adyntel (through treg) takes a store domain, finds the store's own Facebook page and returns its live ads.
  • A store with no ads comes back as a free "complete, 0 ads" (HTTP 204). A hit costs about $0.011.
  • Runs on the queue in parallel, about 100 stores a minute. The old lookup is the fallback.
  • We keep the 10 longest-running ads per store: copy, image, start date, format, landing page.

Done so far

  • 2,454 active Ad Studio users checked: 721 run Meta ads, 1,733 do not.
  • About 1 in 5 stores with a domain runs ads.
  • The TOFU backlog and the Omni stores have not been checked yet.

How the content is made

Voice

A warm, short note (70 to 130 words) from someone who took time on their store. Five beats: a warm opener ("I was going through your ads last night…"), one thing noticed and framed as an easy win, what was made or picked, why it saves time and money compared with starting from scratch or paying an agency, and one link. Subjects are curious and personal ("made 2 ads for your bratwurst 🥩"). The preview text reads like the next line of a friend's message. Signed "Farooq, Growth at Brevo". Written natively in the store's language.

Checks before anything can send

  • Copy lint: banned phrases, length, punctuation, emoji count, link text, no reply asks, no abrupt openers.
  • Fact-check: a second model compares every number, name and claim with the store's data and what each link does.
  • Image QA on generated ads. Anything that fails three rewrites is held back.

Sending, replies, safety

  • Sent one to one through the PushOwl Brevo account (transactional), from sender "Farooq from Ad Studio" <sheik.farooq@pushowl.com>, with a List-Unsubscribe header and a one-click stop link.
  • Replies go to sheik.farooq+ads@pushowl.com. A Gmail filter labels them "Ad Studio" and keeps them out of the inbox.
  • The reply agent is a small Google Apps Script under Farooq's account, running in Google's cloud. It answers in the same thread using a product knowledge base, offers the Calendly call when there is buying intent or setup questions, and stops if they go quiet after our reply. Billing, refunds and complaints go to Farooq.
  • Before every send: blocklist check, unsubscribe and bounce exclusion, weekly cap. Sending auto-pauses on bounces over 2% or complaints over 0.1%.
