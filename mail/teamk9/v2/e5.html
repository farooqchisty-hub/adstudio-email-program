I went through your 30 newest live Meta ads the way a media buyer would. 3 things:

1. 15 of them say "Offer ends soon", and some have said it for over 2 years. People notice. Urgency that never ends stops working.

2. 29 of the 30 buttons say "Shop now". Cheap test: "See sizes" on the harness ads.

3. The harness gets 16 ads, the seat cover gets 6, and both use the same deal-first look.

What I'd do this week: pause the 2025 Black Friday ads, swap "offer ends soon" for a real reason (free size exchanges, 4 metal buckles), and give the seat cover its own 2 or 3 ads about dog hair and muddy paws.
