hey Fleur,

I went through your 30 newest live ads the way a media buyer would. First, you're launching a lot: 16 new ones in the last 30 days. That's great.

3 things I'd change:

1. 12 of the 30 are carousels with "Beserk" as the headline. That's the line people actually read. Put the drop in it, like "Mothman blind boxes are here".

2. Some ads are your Instagram posts word for word, "link in bio" included. Ads have a button, so that line just confuses people.

3. Only a few show a person wearing the piece. In apparel, more than half of the ads running 300+ days are video, and the top ones all put a person on camera.

Quickest win: one try-on video for every new drop. Ad Studio does the presenter 📌
